# Iron Log

A personal, offline-first workout PWA. No accounts, no subscriptions, no backend —
everything (your plan, history, and progress photos) lives in your phone's local storage.

## How it works
1. Upload a plan JSON (see `plan-schema-example.json` for the exact shape) generated by
   Claude or ChatGPT — a full 12–16 week A/B cycle with progressive weights.
2. Tap **Start Workout**. The app always serves the *next* workout in sequence,
   regardless of which day you actually train — miss a day, it just picks up where you left off.
3. Every 3rd completed workout (end of a training week) prompts you for progress photos.
4. The **Stats** tab shows total workouts logged and weight progression per exercise.

## Generating a new plan
Hand this prompt (plus your own numbers) to Claude or ChatGPT:

> Generate a 12-week A/B/A/B strength plan as JSON matching this exact schema: [paste plan-schema-example.json].
> Progress weights by X lb every week on compound lifts, bodyweight reps +1 every 2 weeks, etc.

Then upload the resulting file from the home screen.

## Notes for later
- Exercise icons are currently a generic placeholder figure — swap in per-exercise SVGs
  or a free exercise-image dataset if you want real movement visuals.
- Streak "grace period" (days a gap is forgiven) is set to 4 in `app.js` — change `GRACE_DAYS` to taste.
- Photos and history are stored in IndexedDB only. There's no export/backup yet — worth
  adding before you trust it with 12 weeks of data (a simple "export to JSON" button is a good v2).
